THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know ♡

A few quick answers before your happy mail arrives.

📦 How do I order?

Add items to your bag, open the bag and select checkout. Submit your details to send your order through WhatsApp. The shop will confirm availability and delivery.

💳 How can I pay safely?

Choose your preferred payment method at checkout. The shop will confirm available options and send verified UPI or payment-link details after confirming your order. Never pay an unverified account.

🚚 When will my order arrive?

Delivery charges, stock availability and estimated arrival will be confirmed by the shop before you pay.

♡ Can I change or cancel an order?

Please message the shop as soon as possible. Changes, cancellations, returns and refunds must be confirmed by the shop.
